# First Impression

Flag these early:

1. blurry or screenshot-like plots,
2. unreadable fonts after two-column scaling,
3. missing axis labels or units,
4. legends covering data,
5. line colors that fail in grayscale,
6. inconsistent table precision,
7. captions that do not explain the evidence,
8. diagrams that look hand-drawn or visually inconsistent,
9. figure/table split awkwardly from caption or section title.

These are not cosmetic only. They can signal weak rigor.
