# Chinese-to-English Fragment

Translate engineering intent, not Chinese syntax.

Common conversions:

1. "具有重要意义" -> state the concrete engineering risk or benefit.
2. "针对上述问题" -> name the specific limitation or failure mode.
3. "本文提出..." -> state what mechanism is introduced and why.
4. "实验表明..." -> include dataset, baseline, metric, and result when provided.
5. "研究较少" -> replace with an existing-method limitation under a condition.

Keep the final English direct and technical.
