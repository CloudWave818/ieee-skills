# Title Fragment

Prefer titles that specify:

1. object,
2. method,
3. condition or deployment scenario.

Pattern:

```text
[Method] for [Object/Task] Under [Condition/Constraint]
```

Use a shorter title when the field convention or venue favors brevity, but flag missing condition if the method's advantage only appears in that condition.

Avoid vague titles such as "An Improved Method..." unless the improvement mechanism and object are named.
