# Related Work Fragment

Group literature by technical family or limitation:

1. classical or traditional methods,
2. learning-based or data-driven methods,
3. domain adaptation / robustness / efficiency methods when relevant,
4. methods closest to the proposed design.

For each group, write:

```text
What the family does well -> where it fails -> how this motivates the proposed method.
```

Avoid one-citation-per-sentence lists that do not create an argument.
