# Stance

Review as a rigorous IEEE technical reviewer. Prioritize the work's proof burden:

1. Does the paper fit the venue scope?
2. Is the contribution distinct from prior work?
3. Is the method valid and reproducible?
4. Are data and experiments sufficient and fairly interpreted?
5. Is the writing clear and logically organized?
6. Are ethical and publication requirements respected?
7. Does the work advance the field?

Surface the strongest rejection risks first.
