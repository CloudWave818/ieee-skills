# Method Fragment

Write the method as a reproducible engineering design:

1. Problem definition and notation.
2. Overall architecture or algorithm flow.
3. Core modules and why each exists.
4. Training, optimization, loss, constraint, control law, or signal-processing details.
5. Complexity, deployment, stability, or resource implications when claimed.

Every major design choice should be justified by the object, condition, or limitation. Do not present architecture choices as arbitrary blocks.
