Why It Exists

IEEE manuscript problems are often not only language problems. They are engineering-evidence problems.

Messy research materialsIdeas, code, AI chats, experiment logs, and literature summaries are scattered before writing starts.
Weak motivationAbstracts and introductions omit object, condition, and engineering harm.
Unsupported claimsExperiments exist, but they do not map cleanly to the stated claims.
Unconvincing baselinesComparisons use weak, old, or unfair baselines.
Hidden engineering costAccuracy improves, but latency, complexity, robustness, or deployment cost is missing.
Figure first-impression riskPlots fail after IEEE two-column scaling or captions do not state the takeaway.
AI-like prose or weak reviewsProse sounds template-like, or reviewer comments are generic, contradictory, or unsupported.
Weak revision packageReviewer responses explain instead of showing concrete changes and new evidence.

Engineering Evidence Chain

IEEE-style papers should make the technical problem, constraint, method rationale, and evidence easy to inspect.

ObjectWhat system or task?
ConditionWhat constraint or scenario?
HarmWhy does it matter?
LimitationWhere do prior methods fail?
RationaleWhy this method?
EvidenceWhich results prove it?

Skills

ieee-summarizeConvert messy notes, code, logs, AI chats, literature summaries, and related-work candidates into an IEEE Research Brief.
ieee-writingDraft IEEE-style titles, abstracts, introductions, methods, experiments, conclusions, and contribution statements.
ieee-polishingPolish or translate technical prose into precise IEEE-style English, including AI-like prose repair.
ieee-reviewerAssess manuscripts and reviewer-comment quality with IEEE-style gates: scope, novelty, validity, data, clarity, compliance, and advancement.
ieee-experimentAudit claim-evidence alignment, baselines, ablations, robustness, complexity, and reproducibility.
ieee-figure-tableCheck figure/table readability, captions, accessibility, layout, first-impression risks, and polished IEEE matplotlib house style.
ieee-responseDraft point-by-point responses, rebuttals, revision plans, and cover letters.
ieee-latexFix IEEEtran LaTeX, floats, equations, algorithms, tables, BibTeX, and PDF checks.
ieee-citationCheck BibTeX, reference metadata, IEEE format, DOI fields, and citation logic.
ieee-paper-readerExtract contributions, methods, equations, experiments, limitations, reproducibility details, and citation positioning.

Quick Start

Draft a section ieee-writing Use $ieee-writing to draft an IEEE-style introduction from my problem statement and contributions.
Polish English ieee-polishing Use $ieee-polishing to polish this abstract into concise IEEE-style technical English.
Repair AI-like prose ieee-polishing Use $ieee-polishing to identify AI-like prose and revise it into natural, evidence-grounded IEEE writing.
Pre-review a manuscript ieee-reviewer Use $ieee-reviewer to evaluate this manuscript like an IEEE Transactions reviewer.
Audit experiments ieee-experiment Use $ieee-experiment to build a claim-evidence matrix and tell me which experiments are missing.
