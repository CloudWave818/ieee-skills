# AI-Like Prose

Repair prose that sounds generic, over-smoothed, or template-like while preserving facts.

Check for:

1. repetitive transitions such as "Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally" without logical function,
2. broad claims that could fit any paper,
3. balanced but empty sentence pairs,
4. exaggerated certainty not backed by evidence,
5. generic phrases such as "significantly enhances", "comprehensive framework", or "valuable insights" without metric, condition, or mechanism,
6. loss of author-specific details: dataset, system, constraint, failure case, module name, parameter, baseline, or observed result.

Revision strategy:

1. Restore concrete engineering nouns and conditions.
2. Vary sentence rhythm only when it improves logic.
3. Replace generic praise with measured claims tied to evidence.
4. Keep some concise, direct sentences; do not over-polish every sentence into the same academic cadence.
5. Add missing-evidence notes instead of hiding unsupported claims.

Do not promise to evade AI detectors. Detectors are unreliable and can misclassify fluent human writing. The goal is authentic, source-grounded technical prose.
